Subscription cancellation and undo scheduled subscription cancellation/resume are separate **PRODUCT_DECISION_PENDING** gaps, not scheduled plan/seat operations. No subscription cancellation/resume implementation is added by PAY-04. External administration and display of cancellation status do not prove application support.
