Do not advertise a cancellation control or certify these as supported. The endpoints remain deployed for stable failure contracts.

Subscription cancellation is available through the authenticated Paddle customer portal. Subscription resume remains **PRODUCT_DECISION_PENDING**. Signed subscription updates drive access; opening or closing the portal does not change entitlements.

Deployment must validate the exact reviewed prefix and pending suffix through 188.
