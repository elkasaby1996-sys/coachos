# Paddle-only certification evidence

Canonical scenarios: config/staging-commercial-scenarios.json,31 entries. Explicit scenario-ID/assertion mapping lives in scripts/billing-deployment-contract.mjs. No live LS success, LS portal or resume-through-LS scenario remains.

Distinguish CERTIFIED HISTORICALLY, LOCALLY VERIFIED, MUST RERUN AFTER RETIREMENT DEPLOYMENT, NOT YET CERTIFIED, and UNSUPPORTED / PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED. Historical webhook ingress evidence does not certify the new artifact. Units/SQL/local fixtures do not establish provider certification.

Templates remain not_run/blocked. Pass requires scenario-specific positive assertions, exact commit/environment and real staging_test scope. Missing/failed/blocked/unrelated assertions block before conditional outcomes are considered. Local/non-applicable records cannot yield unconditional pass. Unsupported subscription cancellation/resume cannot be marked pass; a product decision is required.

Publish only allowlisted statuses, timestamps, commit/run identity, static assertion codes and privately salted identifier digests. Never publish secret values, headers, signatures, payloads, capability URLs, customer PII, provider IDs, authorization JSON, inventories or raw CLI/API errors. Backups/envelopes/restore proofs stay private.

Deployment evidence records read-only preflight and exact stages/partial completion. Command success does not certify responses, side effects or finances. Production has a separate sanitized status and never declares live billing ready. Upload only allowlisted result files, not private artifact directories.
